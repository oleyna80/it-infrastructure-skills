## Evidence quality checklist
- Was the activity performed in a real professional context?
- What did the candidate personally do, decide, implement and verify?
- Are dates, role, scope, results and third-party confirmation available?
- Does the evidence match the exact official competence?
- Is there a safe sanitized public summary and a separate private original?

**Do not commit:** contracts, customer names, personal data, proprietary logs, credentials, identifiable network diagrams, or unredacted invoices.

**Important:** a GitHub portfolio is supplementary evidence; VAE eligibility, admissibility, evaluation and final validation are decided by authorized bodies, not by this repository.
